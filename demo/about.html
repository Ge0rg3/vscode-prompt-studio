About Aurora

Aurora is built by four people who met while maintaining an open source markdown parser. We shipped the first version in 2023 after a year of using it ourselves.

We started it because our old notes kept getting stranded. Two apps we relied on shut down, and each export left us with a folder of files that no longer opened cleanly.

Aurora has no format of its own. Your notes are markdown files in a folder you pick, and you can edit them in Aurora, in a text editor, or from a terminal on a server.

We fund the work through one-time licences rather than a subscription. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded.
